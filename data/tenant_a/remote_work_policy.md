# Remote Work Policy

Tenant A employees may work remotely on Mondays and Fridays unless a team lead requests on-site attendance for a scheduled workshop.

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption, screen lock after ten minutes, and the approved password manager for all shared credentials.

When handling customer data outside the office, employees must connect through the corporate VPN before opening internal dashboards or ticketing systems.

Travel reimbursement for home-office equipment is capped at 300 dollars per calendar year and requires manager approval before purchase.
