# Expense Policy

Tenant B reimburses client dinner expenses up to 120 dollars per attendee when the meeting agenda and attendee list are attached to the expense report.

International travel requires finance approval at least five business days before departure and must use the preferred booking portal.

Software subscriptions under 50 dollars per month can be approved by a department manager, while larger recurring purchases require procurement review.

Expense reports are due by the fifth business day of the following month.
