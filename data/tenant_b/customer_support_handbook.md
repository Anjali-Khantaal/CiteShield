# Customer Support Handbook

Tenant B support agents must acknowledge severity-one incidents within fifteen minutes and provide updates every thirty minutes until mitigation is in place.

Refund requests for annual contracts can only be approved by the billing operations team after account usage is reviewed.

Escalations involving data exports should be routed to the platform support queue and tagged with the requesting region.

Weekend coverage rotates weekly, and the on-call support lead is responsible for publishing the handoff summary before Monday at 9 AM local time.
